**Date:** November 3, 2026  
**Author:** Juan Cruz Peña

El País, Spain's most-read general-interest newspaper, covered our [report](/publication/r_2026_energytag_spaindatacenterdecree/) with EnergyTag on Spain's draft royal decree for data centers. The decree would require new data centers to back their consumption hour by hour with electricity from new renewable and storage installations.
